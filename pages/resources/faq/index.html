What is Computer-on-Module COM Express Type 7?

COM Express Type 7 is a PICMG standard targeting high-performance server-grade processing at the edge. Unlike Type 6 which focuses on display outputs, Type 7 replaces graphic interfaces with up to four high-speed 10G/25G Ethernet ports and extra PCIe lanes to expand network logic resources.

Does Senao Networks support custom ODM design?

Yes. Senao Networks has 30+ years of experiences in hardware, software, layout design, and RF tuning. We support custom BIOS, custom chassis/thermal design, network port expansion, and manufacturing verification for carrier-grade appliances.

What quality certification standards do your plants support?

Our global manufacturing sites support ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001, and TL 9000. All products pass strict regulatory testings including CE, FCC, RoHS, and WEEE compliance prior to shipment.
