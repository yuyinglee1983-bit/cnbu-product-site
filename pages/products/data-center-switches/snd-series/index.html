Key Features

Ultra-High Density 400GbE

32-port QSFP-DD 400GbE in a 1U form factor delivers the highest port density per rack unit, enabling massive bandwidth for hyperscale and enterprise data center deployments without sacrificing footprint.

Industry-Leading Low Latency

Sub-600 ns cut-through switching enables real-time financial trading, HPC cluster interconnects, and AI training workloads that demand deterministic, microsecond-level response times across the fabric.
