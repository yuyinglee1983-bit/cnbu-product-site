Why Senao Computing

Senao Networks is headquartered in Hwaya Technology Park, Taoyuan, Taiwan, with an R&D center in Nangang, Taipei.
With over 1,200 employees worldwide, R&D engineers account for 60% of our workforce, and R&D investment reaches up to 8% of annual revenue.
We also have sales, technical support, and R&D operations in the US, Japan, and India to provide global coverage and local support.
